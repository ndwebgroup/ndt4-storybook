## Usage

<ul className="list--todo">
  <li>
    ### Dos
    <ul>
      <li>Use for a single focused action — apply, visit, give, register.</li>
      <li>Keep titles short and verb-driven; the title is the call to action.</li>
      <li>Choose a background that contrasts with the surrounding section.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>
    ### Donts
    <ul>
      <li>Do not place more than one CTA Card in the same view.</li>
      <li>Do not put links inside the summary — the whole card is already a link.</li>
      <li>Avoid long summaries; two short sentences at most.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>

## Accessibility

- The whole card is a single `<a>` element, so it is one tab stop with one accessible name — keep the title meaningful on its own.
- The arrow is decorative (`aria-hidden`); no interaction is lost without it.
- Text colors resolve through `light-dark()` against both dark and light card backgrounds.
