## Usage

The Video Card is a standard card that uses a video in place of the card image. The video thumbnail includes a play button, and selecting it opens the video in a modal dialog so visitors can watch without leaving the page. Use it in card listings and grids to highlight videos alongside other content.

## Accessibility

The video thumbnail is a link that opens a native `dialog` element, so focus moves into the dialog when it opens and the close button and the Escape key both dismiss it. Closing the dialog pauses the video. Videos should include captions, and the card title should describe the video.
